SEM 6

6th Semester · Co-Authored Research Paper

Experimental Investigation on Wear Behaviour of SLM-Fabricated Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) by Pin-on-Disc Tribometer

✓ Completed RSM-CCD Design ANOVA R² = 90.25% ASTM G99-95a Pin-on-Disc

Systematic tribological investigation of SLM-fabricated Titanium Grade 23 against EN31 steel counter-face. Applied Response Surface Methodology with Central Composite Design across 20 experimental runs. Input variables: applied load (30–50 N), sliding speed (1–2 m/s), sliding distance (1000–2000 m). ANOVA confirmed sliding distance as dominant factor contributing ~73% to WML. Developed quadratic regression predictive models. Identified inverse CoF–speed relationship attributed to in-situ TiO₂ rutile tribo-oxide formation acting as solid boundary lubricant at extreme conditions. Optimum parameters identified: 30 N · 1.0 m/s · 1000 m.
